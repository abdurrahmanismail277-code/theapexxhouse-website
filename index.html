          THE APEX HOUSE • APEX CONSULTING

          Everything you need to know about Apex Consulting

          Where Excellence Meets Expression.

          Every successful event begins with a clear direction. An event is more than a date, a venue, and a list of activities. It is an experience designed to achieve a purpose. When you have the vision but need structure to bring it to life, questions about direction, audience experience, programme structure, and execution can feel overwhelming.

          Apex Consulting helps event organisers move from uncertainty to clarity and from ideas to intentional planning. We provide strategic guidance to help you think through your event, make informed decisions, and develop it with a clearer sense of direction. Whether you need clarity before you begin or professional guidance throughout planning, we help you build with intention.

          Because an event shouldn’t just happen. It should have a clear purpose and a well-considered direction.

          01 • FOCUSED CONSULTATION

          Event Clarity Session

          ₦8,000  |  60 minutes

          Sometimes, you don’t need someone to plan your entire event. You need someone to help you see the bigger picture. This focused consultation helps you think through your event, identify areas that need attention, and gain clarity on your next steps.

          We explore your vision, discuss your concerns, and consider the decisions necessary to move your event forward.

          What to expect

          • A focused discussion about your event idea and objectives.
          • Guidance on areas where you need greater clarity.
          • Strategic insights to help you evaluate your plans.
          • Clearer direction on the next steps to consider.

          Who is this for?

          Event organisers with an idea or event in development who need professional guidance before proceeding.

          The outcome: A clearer perspective on your event and a better understanding of the steps ahead.

          02 • ONGOING GUIDANCE

          Event Advisory

          ₦35,000 / 1 month  |  ₦65,000 / 2 months  |  ₦100,000 / 3 months

          Some events require more than a single conversation. As plans develop, new questions emerge, decisions need to be evaluated, and different aspects of the event must work together. Event Advisory provides ongoing strategic consultation to help you navigate these stages with greater clarity and intention.

          You have access to professional guidance as you work towards bringing your event vision to life. We help you think through challenges, evaluate your direction, and make informed decisions as planning progresses.

          What this service offers

          • Ongoing strategic consultations.
          • Guidance on event development and planning decisions.
          • Support in evaluating your event’s direction and objectives.
          • Professional input as questions and challenges arise.

          Who is this for?

          Event organisers who want continued strategic guidance as they develop and prepare their events.

          The outcome: A more considered approach to event planning, with professional guidance supporting your decisions along the way.

          Turn your project goals into a clear plan and keep the moving parts coordinated. We help organize priorities, timelines, contributors, and follow-through so your project keeps moving toward delivery.

          Ask us directly
          What services does The Apex House offer?
          We offer event moderation, launch hosting, community management and public speaking. We’ll shape the support around your goals and the people you want to reach.
          Can I book you for a single event?
          Yes. Get in touch with your event date, location and the kind of support you need. We’ll discuss availability and a suitable plan.
          How do we get started?
          Send us a short note about your event or project. We’ll arrange a conversation, learn what matters to you and outline the next steps.
          Do you support both in-person and online programs?
          Tell us what format you have in mind. We can talk through your audience, program flow and the kind of hosting or moderation that will work best.
          How far in advance should I reach out?
          As soon as you have a date or an idea taking shape. Early conversations give us more room to plan together; you’re welcome to ask about shorter timelines too.
